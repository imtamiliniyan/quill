Quill

Dictation that never
leaves your Mac.

A free, on-device replacement for paid dictation tools like Wispr Flow and Gladio. Hold a key, speak, release — transcribed locally on the Neural Engine and typed straight into whatever's focused. No subscription, no account, no audio ever sent anywhere.

No spam. One email when it's ready to download.

🔒

Runs entirely on-device

Transcription happens on your Mac's Neural Engine. Nothing you say is ever uploaded — not for transcription, not for analytics, not for anything.

⚡

Push-to-talk, everywhere

Hold the hotkey in any text field — Messages, a browser, a terminal, Slack — speak, release. The transcript types itself in, usually within 200–300ms.

📊

Your own dictation history

Every dictation, word counts, speaking speed, streaks — all computed from a plain file on your Mac. Nothing here is sent anywhere either.

💸

Free. No subscription.

No word limits, no per-seat pricing, no "upgrade to Pro" wall. Quill is free and will stay that way.

Your data stays on your Mac. Quill doesn't have a server. There's nothing to send your dictation to, so it never does.
